# Part 2 — AbleSpace: Take Data (Caseload)

> Outline only. Fill each section from your own walkthrough — the brief asks
> for the workflow **in your own words**, and a reviewer who works on this
> product every day will spot a description written from the outside.

## 1. What this screen is for

One paragraph: whose job is this, what are they trying to get done, and under
what conditions (mid-session, on a tablet, one hand free?).

## 2. How I got there

Navigation path from login to Take Data. Screenshot each step.

## 3. The workflow, step by step

For each step: a screenshot, what the user does, what the system does back,
and what state changes as a result. Call out anything that surprised you.

## 4. What the design gets right

Be specific. "Clean UI" says nothing; "the goal selector keeps the last-used
goal expanded so a repeat session takes two taps instead of five" says you
actually looked.

## 5. Improvements I'd propose

Strongest section for the "product thinking" criterion. For each item:

| Observation | Why it costs the user | Proposed change | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Rules of thumb that make this land:
- Tie each one to the user's real constraint (mid-session, hands busy, a child
  waiting), not to a generic heuristic.
- Distinguish UI polish from functionality gaps, and say which you'd do first.
- Include at least one you decided *against* proposing, with the reason. It
  shows judgment rather than a list of everything you noticed.

## 6. Open questions

Things you couldn't determine from the outside and would ask the team.
